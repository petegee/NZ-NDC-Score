# Story — Competitor sign-up invites (reusable people, not placeholders)

**Status:** Backlog — investigation · **Raised:** 2026-09-17

## What

Investigate the flow where the pilots on an NDC entry sheet are **invited by
email to sign up with Soarscore**, so the person record the organiser created
on the night carries a real (verified) email and, eventually, a bound
identity. Deliverable is a **decision + plan, not a feature**: does this earn
its keep, where does each piece live (NdcScore UI vs Soarscore vs neither),
and whether it forces the deliberate, narrow reopening of "no NdcScore
backend" for mail sending.

## Why it matters

Every unknown pilot tonight becomes a placeholder person — `unknown@mfnz.invalid`
(per `deferred-decisions.md`, 2026-09-17). That is fine for capture but
accumulates waste:

- email search (`GET /people?email=`) can never find them;
- identity binding (`/link-sign-in` linking is by verified email) is
  impossible for them — they can never sign in as themselves;
- next season the same pilot may be re-created as a second person when the
  organiser's name autocomplete misses a spelling variant.

Re-use is the point of the person store: returning pilots should surface by
autocomplete (or email) and get captured without re-registration. Note the
cheap fallback that needs no new machinery: if the organiser learns a real
email, `POST /change-person-contact-details` upgrades the record tonight.
This story is about what happens *before* the organiser has the email — the
competitor supplies it.

## Before starting

- Read `SoarScore2/kanban/completed/authentication-and-authorisation.md` —
  the sign-in/linking model already exists: `/link-sign-in` takes no body
  (identity from the token; auto-links by **verified** email; 409
  `auth.signIn.explicitLinkRequired` → organiser resolves via
  `/bind-identity`), 403 `auth.signIn.emailNotVerified`, 403
  `auth.contact.emailOwnership` (a caller cannot claim an email another
  identity owns). Any sign-up flow must fit those codes, not fight them.
- `deferred-decisions.md` in this repo: no-backend/BFF, email-out-of-MVP,
  placeholder-email (whose "revisit" clause this story exercises).
- `kanban/backlog/email-results.md` — the other mail question; align so the
  two stories make the same call on sending (or knowingly diverge).
- Check what sign-in surface already exists on the Soarscore side (its
  `wwwroot`, the auth story's UI work) — a competitor sign-up page may
  already be half-built there.

## Investigation questions (as WIs)

- **WI-1 — Model the target state.** What does a "signed-up competitor" mean
  in Soarscore today: identity bound + email verified on the person record?
  Can a person self-serve an email change (the `auth.contact.emailOwnership`
  flow), or is it organiser-mediated? Is there a person-merge, and if two
  placeholder persons exist for one pilot, how do they collapse — new
  Soarscore capability or organiser manual fix? Deliverable: a short written
  model + gaps list, in this story file.
- **WI-2 — Invitation delivery.** Who sends the invite, given the SPA cannot
  send mail: (a) mailto: batch / copy-paste invite text generated by NdcScore
  (zero backend, same stance as `email-results.md`'s copy-for-email), (b)
  the organiser's own mail-merge, (c) server-side sending on Soarscore —
  the narrow reopening `deferred-decisions.md` contemplates. Deliverable: a
  recommendation with the reopening question made explicit for Pete.
- **WI-3 — Sign-up surface + placeholder upgrade.** Where does the
  competitor actually sign up (Soarscore-hosted page? IdP-hosted? none yet),
  and what is the minimum flow that turns a placeholder person into a
  verified one: sign-up with the email → organiser-invoked binding? invite
  token carrying the person id? Compare against simply doing nothing (the
  fallback above). Deliverable: the chosen shape, or a reasoned "not yet".
- **WI-4 — Record the decision.** Write the outcome into
  `deferred-decisions.md` and, if it is a go, raise the follow-up stories in
  the right repos (Soarscore-side changes are Soarscore stories; NdcScore
  invites UI is this repo's). Investigation stories end in board hygiene,
  not code.

## Verification

The story is done when the decision is recorded with enough evidence that a
future reader can disagree with it constructively: the model from WI-1, the
delivery recommendation from WI-2, and the shape from WI-3 — or an explicit
"no-go, the fallback suffices" with the reasoning.
